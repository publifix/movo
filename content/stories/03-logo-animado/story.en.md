# Your logo can't stand still anymore

::act
::scene s03-still

## A still logo in a world that moves

Your logo was designed for a business card, a sign or the corner of a document. Today it lives somewhere
else: the start of a reel, the end of an ad, the cover of a presentation, an app's loading screen, a stage
with LED screens.

In all those places, everything moves except your logo. It pops in, sits there and pops out. And in a feed
where every second competes for attention, a logo that does nothing is a logo nobody sees.

Animating a logo isn't adding effects. It's answering a question most brands never ask: **how does your
brand move?**

### Where an animated logo works

- **At the start and end of your videos and reels**, so every piece ends with your signature.
- **In presentations and talks**, on the cover and in the transitions between sections.
- **On your website or app**, as a loading screen or a detail on the home page.
- **At events and on LED screens**, where a static logo gets lost on a stage that moves.
- **In webinars, livestreams and video calls**, as an intro and outro.

::act
## A logo is also a behavior

::scene s03-cover

When a logo moves, something new appears: the way it enters, reacts and signs off. That behavior says as
much as its colors or its typeface.

A financial brand might enter with precision: straight lines, exact timing, nothing out of place. A drinks
brand might bounce. A fashion brand might appear slowly, with calm and space. A tech brand might arrive fast,
with cuts and energy. The same logo, animated four different ways, tells four different stories.

That's why a good animated logo starts with strategy, not software: what should someone feel in the three
seconds it lasts?

::act
## Choose your brand's personality

Type your brand's name and try how it would look with each personality. It's a quick sketch in type; your
real logo is animated from its own design, shapes and colors.

::logo

### Precise

Clean moves, straight angles, exact timing. It says order and trust. It suits financial services, B2B tech,
healthcare, law firms and consultancies.

### Playful

Bounces, stretch, a touch of exaggeration. It says warmth and good energy. It suits food and drinks, kids'
products, consumer apps and brands that want to smile.

### Elegant

Slow entrances, fades, lots of space. It says calm, quality and exclusivity. It suits fashion, beauty,
hospitality, real estate and wine.

### Energetic

Fast cuts, a strong beat, impact. It says strength and speed. It suits sports, events, music, consumer tech
and launches.

## What you get with an animated logo

An animated logo lasts 3 to 6 seconds and is made to open and close your videos, presentations and social
posts. To work everywhere, it's delivered as:

- **Full and short versions.** The full one to open a video; the short one for outros, stories and places
  where every second counts.
- **Vertical, square and horizontal.** 9:16 for Reels, TikTok and Stories; 1:1 and 4:5 for the feed; 16:9 for
  YouTube, your website and presentations.
- **Transparent background.** So your team can place it over any video or photo without a box around it.

And in the formats each use needs: social-optimized MP4, high-quality files for screens, and Lottie, a light
format that lets your animated logo live inside your website or app without weighing like a video.

### How long does it take?

An animated logo is one of the quickest pieces to produce. Depending on how complex your logo is and the
personality you're after, we can deliver it finished in a couple of days. Before animating, we show you the
motion proposal for approval, and the animation includes two rounds of revisions, as in any project.

### Do I need to redesign my logo to animate it?

Not necessarily. Your logo is animated from its current design: its shapes, letters and colors are the raw
material. Sometimes animating it reveals details worth adjusting so it looks good in motion or at small
sizes, like a very thin stroke or an element that gets lost on a phone. If so, we propose it before
animating, and your logo is still your logo.
